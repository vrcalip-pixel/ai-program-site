    AI 40 · Formerly COSA 55

    AI and Machine Learning Foundations

    3 unitsNo prerequisitesCounts toward AI Literacy

    What this course is

    Your starting point. You'll learn what AI and machine learning actually are — and aren't — and where they already show up in business, marketing, education, and healthcare. Then you'll get hands-on with AI-powered tools and learn to judge them: what they're good at, where they fail, and what responsible use looks like in a real workplace.

    What you'll build

    • 1
      An AI tool evaluationPick a tool your workplace could use, test it against a real task, and write up whether it's worth adopting.
    • 2
      A bias and privacy auditTrace one AI system's data, decisions, and blind spots — and what you'd change.
    • 3
      A responsible-AI integration planA short, defensible proposal for bringing AI into a team or organization.
