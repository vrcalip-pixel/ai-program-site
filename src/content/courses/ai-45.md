Generative AI writes, draws, summarizes and drafts. This course explains how it does that in plain terms, and then teaches you to direct it with intent. You'll move past one-line requests to prompts you construct deliberately, test against a goal, critique, and refine until the output is something you'd put your name on.

You'll also take the ethics seriously. Every generative system carries the biases of its data and raises real questions about privacy and consent. You'll learn to see those issues before they become problems, and to explain them to the people you work with.
