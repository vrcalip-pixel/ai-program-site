Real work isn't only text. It's images, recordings, video, and documents, usually mixed together. This course is about AI systems that work across all of them: describing an image in words, searching a video library by speaking, or building an assistant you can talk to.

You'll design, test and deploy those prototypes with no-code and low-code tools, and you'll keep asking who the system is for and what could go wrong. It ends with a portfolio-ready project you built for a real workplace or community need.
