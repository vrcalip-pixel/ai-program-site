Automation is easy to start and hard to trust. This course teaches you to build workflows where AI does the repetitive work and a person stays accountable for the outcome. You'll connect language models, multi-modal tools and the productivity apps people already use, and streamline processes that actually exist.

Along the way you'll learn task design, how to orchestrate several agents, how retrieval-augmented generation keeps answers grounded in real documents, and how to evaluate a workflow with humans in the loop. The goal is automation that is ethical, reliable and equitable, for business, education and community settings.
