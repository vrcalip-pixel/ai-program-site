This is the capstone. Working alone or in a team, you'll take a real-world problem and carry it all the way: analyze it, design an applied AI workflow, build a functional prototype with no-code and low-code tools, and deploy it. The solution has to integrate what you've learned, generative, conversational and multi-modal systems working together.

The studio is also where you learn to finish like a professional: ethical design decisions written down, documentation someone else could follow, analytics that show whether it works, and a final portfolio and presentation aimed at workforce or community impact.
